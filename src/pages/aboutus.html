SilipLokal

SilipLokal helps people discover Filipino local brands, products, and businesses.

It aims to make homegrown businesses easier to find and learn about, and to encourage support for local entrepreneurship.

SilipLokal brings local businesses into one place, making it easier to explore the products and makers in Filipino communities.

By helping homegrown brands become more visible and accessible, the platform invites shoppers to discover and support local entrepreneurship.

Supporting Local Businesses

Discover local businesses, explore what they make, and learn more about the people and ideas behind each brand.

Every discovery is an opportunity to make homegrown businesses more visible and accessible. Explore local brands and find new ways to support Filipino entrepreneurship.
